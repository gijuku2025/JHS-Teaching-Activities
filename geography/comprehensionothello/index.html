South America Othello

Lay the iPad flat between you in landscape. Player 1 (black) sits at the left edge, Player 2 (white) at the right. Each player's passage, question and answers face their own seat. Read the passage, then answer: a correct answer lets you place a bead, a wrong answer loses your turn. The game ends when the board is full or neither player can move, and the player with more beads wins.
